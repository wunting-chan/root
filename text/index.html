Wun Ting Chan (Ting)

Wun Ting Chan (Ting) is an artist, translator, and computer scientist from Hong Kong, based in New York.

This is a fast, text-first entrance to the portfolio. The same work is also explorable as a 3D computer-lab experience.

Selected work

  • InFlux

    Performance · video · machine learning · completed

  • Evading Online Keyword Censorship (PixelStacks)

    Research · software · steganography · completed

    Sensitive information, such as banned books and protest organization communications, require secure channels to evade private or state surveillance. This project aims to provide public access to a secure steganographic system for additional privacy in communications and underground publishing.

  • Temporary Autonomous Zone — Traditional Chinese Translation

    2020 · Translation · book · civic action · completed

    5,000 copies of the translated text were made available for free in Hong Kong in 2020 with the help of 40+ volunteers.

  • eliza

    Web-based art · ongoing

    eliza is a web-based art project that lures participants into a mesmerizing maze-like website.

  • humanjuices

    Web design · completed

    This is a website created for the artist Giannina Gomez who is a New York City-based fashion designer, performer, and puppeteer specializing in ironically sustainable fashion made from textile waste.

  • Sculpture Work

    2019–2022 · Sculpture · completed

    Started with modeling a strange creature with polycephaly, a condition where an animal grew more than one head. This body of work was shown in various group exhibits in 2019 and 2022.
