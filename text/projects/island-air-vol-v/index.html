Island Air Vol V: Wilderness

A wilderness-themed group art exhibit featuring the works of 16 visual, sound, and performance artists from the Pacific Northwest area, organized to be concurrent with the 2019 Seattle Art Fair.

Project Statement

I collaborated with artist Aubrey Birdwell to produce a billboard advertisement for "wilderness". We also produced part of the costume for performance artist Butylene O'Kipple. Besides participating as an artist, I also designed the flyers and related promotion material and helped with installation at the show.

Process

My contribution to the show included the flyer design, the piece Weekender, process documentation, and a headpiece for performance artist Butylene O'Kipple.
